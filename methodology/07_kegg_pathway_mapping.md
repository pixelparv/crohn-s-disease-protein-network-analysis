# 7. KEGG pathway mapping and overlap

## Purpose

Locate the target genes within their respective KEGG pathways and identify their positions in the pathway networks.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Tool | KEGG Mapper Color |
| Organism | *Homo sapiens* (hsa) |
| Mapping date | 19 June 2026 |

## Approach

Target/network genes were mapped to KEGG pathways directed to hsa (_Homo sapiens_) using KEGG Mapper's Color tool. Identified hit genes were highlighted distinctly from other genes in the pathway networks to distinguish the experimental genes of interest.

## Interpretation

The locations of the target genes within the KEGG pathway networks were identified and visualized. Highlighting the hit genes enables their positions to be examined in the context of the surrounding pathway components.
