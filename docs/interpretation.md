# Interpretation and limitations

## What the workflow can support

- A transparent summary of selected literature-associated candidate genes.
- A database-derived protein association network under stated thresholds.
- Descriptive network topology and hub rankings for that particular graph.
- Statistical pathway-enrichment results and pathway membership overlap.

## What it cannot establish by itself

- That a protein causes Crohn’s disease.
- That an enriched pathway is activated or suppressed.
- That every STRING edge is a direct physical interaction.
- That a hub is necessarily a therapeutic target.
- That pathway overlap proves functional crosstalk.
- That the findings are clinically validated.

## Reporting language

Prefer “associated with,” “ranked as a hub in this network,” “over-represented,” and “shared pathway membership.” Avoid “causes,” “activates,” “inhibits,” or “validated target” unless supported by independent evidence.
